# Next-day reconstruction — fixed follow-up scope, 20 October 2026

The original question is next-day information, not next-minute trading. Earlier minute results neither prove nor disprove next-day predictability. This is a new exploratory specification after extensive prior work on the archive; 2020–2021 are held out from this implementation, not guaranteed untouched research data.

Use the 483 stocks selected using 2017 coverage in the minute study, retaining missing/delisted names without a test-coverage screen. Rebuild daily summaries from original 2017–2019 raw-derived caches and raw 2020–2021 books. Freeze signal information at 15:50. Evaluate three clearly timed price-return targets: today 15:55 to next trading day 09:35; next trading day 09:35 to 15:55; today 15:55 to next trading day 15:55. These are near-close/near-open proxies, not official auction prices. Do not jump over missing sessions to find a convenient next observation. Exclude shortened/irregular sessions and log their effect.

Primary target is next-session 09:35–15:55. Overnight and combined targets are secondary because corporate actions and dividends are not independently verified. Exclude absolute overnight log gaps >=0.20 for those two targets and log every exclusion; this can remove genuine extreme moves and limits inference to the retained sample. They remain unadjusted price-return targets, not total returns. Keep all finite intraday outcomes; use robust training preprocessing and report sensitivity to extreme outcomes.

Fit on signal dates 2017–2018 (labels must mature before 2019), choose bounded model settings on 2019 (labels must mature before 2020), refit through 2019, evaluate 2020 and 2021 separately and jointly. No 2020/2021 outcome selects settings. Baseline features include past returns at several horizons, price volatility/range, spread/depth, market/sector return context and trailing realized target means using only matured outcomes. Added book features describe persistent vs transient imbalance, morning/afternoon/late pressure, sampled endpoint flow, spread-scaled imbalance, pressure relative to price movement, and common/sector pressure. Sector labels are the project's static mapping, not a verified point-in-time classification; report this limitation. Genuine depth summaries are a separate matched ten-level sensitivity, not padded into one-level stocks.

Bounded models: ridge, additive spline ridge, histogram gradient boosting, shallow random forest, and a small neural network for regression; logistic and histogram gradient boosting for classification of positive returns. Use identical rows and model families for baseline versus baseline+book. At most two candidate settings per family, fixed random seed, training-only imputation/clipping/scaling, no shuffled cross-validation or test-period stopping. Include zero, training mean and rolling own-target mean controls. Pooled models share statistical strength; ticker-specific trailing means control persistent differences.

Evaluate daily paired MSE differences, rank information coefficients, classification log loss/Brier/accuracy, and top-20 directional portfolios plus long-10/short-10 rankings. Prices at entry/exit determine explicit spread-crossing P&L. Compare the same rule with baseline forecasts, retain cash for absent signals, do not rank using future outcomes, and report missing selected outcomes. Predictions and target dates are saved for replay. Day-block uncertainty and multiplicity adjustment cover the planned model-family increments; classify additional analyses as exploratory. Fees/impact/borrow/capacity remain unmodeled, and no passive fills are inferred. A negative result limits this tested family, not every possible regression or classifier.

Implementation clarification before test-result inspection: Holm correction covers seven primary next-session increments and fourteen secondary-target increments separately. The two matched depth increments (ridge and boosting) form a separate sensitivity family. Extreme-outcome diagnostics retain |next-session log return| < 0.10 or omit March 2020; these are descriptive checks, not alternative primary samples. Missing static sector labels become Unknown.
