# Decision-stage extension — frozen 21 October 2026

Purpose: decide whether to pitch a positive research hypothesis to Mihai, pivot, or stop. Prior 2019 minute and 2020–2021 daily outcomes have been inspected; all extensions on those dates are exploratory. Do not optimize against the new earlier test results.

1. Audit the existing combined-logistic top20 lead: paired baseline, directional/sector exposure, same-universe benchmark attribution, contribution concentration, extreme-outcome sensitivity, costs and multiplicity across all previously reported 84 portfolio variants (3 targets × 7 families × 2 feature groups × 2 rules). Benchmark attribution is diagnostic, not an investable portfolio or causal alpha estimate. Preserve missing-outcome exclusions.
2. Historical chronological check: daily summaries 2011–2016; universe uses at least 200 valid daily signals in 2011, excluding the same ETF list. Train 2011–2013, validate 2014, refit through 2014, evaluate 2015–2016 separately and pooled. This is a separate earlier period, not a prospective holdout and not guaranteed unexamined by the original project. Archive composition can be survivorship-biased. Freeze the combined logistic top20 specification; compare matching baseline and book and neutral long10/short10. Use existing feature definitions and fixed two-setting validation choice. No test-year retuning.
3. Economically aligned extension: intraday and combined returns in excess of contemporaneous cross-stock mean (training labels only), ridge/spline ridge regression and logistic classification. Baseline versus book; raw-return cash P&L still scored. Four fixed portfolio variants: top20 signed / long10-short10, each unrestricted or restricted to the lower half of current signal spreads. Selection uses only signal information. Rank classifiers around 0.5. Select one family/rule per target on validation net mean with cash available; report every variant and cash selection. This is a small explicit search, not unrestricted model mining. Fit 2017–2018, validate 2019, test 2020–2021 descriptively; independently repeat chronology above and freeze before inspecting 2015–2016.
4. Separate risk pivot: forecast next-session log realized volatility, log price range and log mean spread with ridge and histogram boosting; identical baseline/book comparisons, same chronological splits. Do not call risk prediction trading alpha. Six increments form a separate Holm family; require directional agreement across the two periods before proposing a risk project. Realized quantities end at the next 15:50 signal cutoff.

Inference: daily paired losses, 20-day circular bootstrap, 2,000 draws. Historical primary combined-logistic book-minus-baseline loss and top20 P&L increments are two corrected comparisons. Economic model losses have six increments per period (2 targets × 3 families); portfolio exploration must be explicitly multiplicity-qualified, and validation-selected strategy must beat cash and its matching baseline in the earlier test before a positive recommendation. No claim of event-OFI replication, total-return accuracy, passive fills, capacity or verified novelty. Corporate actions remain an overnight limitation; gap-threshold and outlier checks are diagnostics, not adjustment.

Decision standard: a positive research lead needs evidence beyond matching price/liquidity controls, stability across years/periods, and robustness to costs/exposure/concentration, plus a specific distinction from prior literature. Failure supports stopping this snapshot directional-alpha route, while retaining reproducible negative results. A statistically positive risk extension is a different project and needs its own novelty assessment.

Interpretation guard: subtracting realized dollar market/sector exposure is attribution, not proof the removed component is spurious. A book signal could in principle time common returns. The independent earlier test, paired baseline and exposure decomposition must therefore be read together. The risk pivot also overlaps established order-book/volatility/liquidity literature; a positive coefficient alone does not establish novelty.

Additional explicitly exploratory diagnostic (specified before its conditional results): replay the existing 2019 minute baseline/book forecasts and compare core versus close within the same stock-day and matched liquidity states. Stock-specific 2017 cutpoints define spread terciles, trailing-30-minute volatility terciles and depth halves (18 bins). Weight each common bin by the smaller core/close count; use identical bin weights for both sessions. Report own-session relative loss reductions and a common-error-scale difference. This probes a composition/denominator explanation of the original closing effect; it does not create an independent holdout or establish a causal closing mechanism.
